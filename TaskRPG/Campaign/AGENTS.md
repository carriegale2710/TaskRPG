# Campaign Agent Rules

## Scope and source of truth

- Follow the root `AGENTS.md` and `PRD.md`; the PRD owns game mechanics.
- This existing `Campaign/` folder is the current campaign's legacy location. Do not move or bulk-restructure its notes without the user's approval.
- A world may have multiple campaigns. Each campaign owns its plot, arc/project progress, quest board and story timeline. Shared world canon lives in `World/`; do not promote a campaign event to shared canon without approval.
- Shared hero progression (HP, MP, XP, skills, habits and inventory) belongs to `Engine/` and is recalculated from the event log. Never hand-edit or infer those values here.

## Notes in this folder

- `00 Hero.md` is the stable character profile only. Preserve identity details; do not put live stats, skill levels, inventory, active quests or campaign progress in it.
- `01 Campaign Rules.md` contains campaign fiction and world-specific canon, not alternate game mechanics. Use the PRD for mechanics.
- `02 Story So Far.md` is this campaign's summary and timeline. Do not mix in another campaign's events.
- `03 Quest Board.md` is this campaign's view of quests. Each quest links to its real TaskNotes task; never duplicate or edit the task's completion state from story prose.
- `04 Rewards & Loot.md` may describe story rewards, but the engine owns the authoritative inventory and any mechanical perks.

## Writing and continuity

- Preserve existing facts and formatting. Do not invent conflicting canon, advance unrelated plot threads, or overwrite an existing story result.
- Ask before creating, moving, renaming or deleting notes, or changing established character/world facts.
- Keep campaign-specific character memories scoped to this campaign. Shared facts belong in the appropriate character or world profile only when approved.
- Any vault content supplied to an AI provider may be sent to that provider. No note or folder is private by guarantee; do not treat this folder as a confidentiality boundary.
