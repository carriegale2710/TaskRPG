# World Agent Rules

## Purpose and scope

- `World/` contains canon shared by campaigns in this setting: world rules, regions, factions and reusable NPC facts.
- Campaign-specific arcs, quest status, story outcomes and timelines belong to that campaign's folder, not to shared world lore.
- Existing `World/Quests/` notes are legacy campaign material. Leave them in place until an approved migration; do not add new campaign-specific quests there.
- Follow the root `AGENTS.md` and `PRD.md`. Game mechanics and player stats are never defined by world notes.

## Canon and writing

- Preserve established facts and link related world notes with wikilinks.
- Do not invent conflicting world canon, a new faction or a major NPC without the user's direction.
- A campaign event changes shared world canon only when the user explicitly approves that promotion. Keep unapproved outcomes in that campaign's own notes.
- Ask before moving, renaming or deleting world notes, or changing established NPC roles, factions or relationships.
- Stable character profiles belong in `Characters/`; campaign-specific relationship developments stay in the campaign's notes unless approved as shared canon.

## Model visibility

- World notes are not private. Any content supplied to an AI provider may be sent to that provider, and no vault folder or ignore rule guarantees confidentiality.
- Use only the world context needed for the current interaction. Folder and write restrictions protect organization and canon integrity, not privacy.
